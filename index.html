How a placement works

Four steps, and only one of them is yours.

1

We measure your floor

Width, depth, height, and the doorway the cabinet has to pass through. The unit is chosen to fit the shop, not the other way round.

2

We place the freezer

A glass-door merchandising unit rated for a frozen hold, with a digital exterior display so the temperature is readable without opening the door.

3

We stock and restock

Product arrives frozen and goes straight in. Restock cadence is set by what actually moves in your shop.

4

You sell it

Your counter, your customers. Terms are agreed per shop before anything is delivered.

Packs are counted, not weighed

Every pack is a count of cubes. A cube is a cube — so what a customer picks up off the shelf is the same portion every time, and restock is countable at a glance.

For shop owners

What you are actually agreeing to.

Floor space and power

A merchandising freezer needs a footprint, clearance, and a dedicated outlet. We confirm all three on site before anything ships.

No cold-chain burden

You are not receiving, tempering, or re-freezing anything. Product arrives frozen and goes into a unit already holding temperature.

Terms in writing

Placement terms, restock cadence and commercial arrangement are agreed per shop and put in writing before delivery. No handshake deals.

Pilot first

The programme opens as a small pilot. Early shops shape how it runs, and we would rather get three right than thirty wrong.
